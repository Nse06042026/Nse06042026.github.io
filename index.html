FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide admission guidance across India?

Yes. We provide admission guidance across India, with a strong focus on Bangalore and Chennai.

Which courses do you guide students for?

We provide guidance for Engineering, Medical & NEET, Management, Allied Health Sciences and other UG & PG admission pathways.

Can students with lower entrance-exam ranks contact you?

Yes. We can help eligible students understand alternative college, course and admission options.

Do you help with NEET admissions?

Yes. We provide guidance regarding NEET-based medical admissions and applicable counselling/admission processes.

Do you help with direct or institution-level admissions?

We provide guidance regarding available institution-level options, subject to eligibility, admission rules and seat availability.

Do you guarantee admission?

Admission is subject to eligibility, institutional rules, seat availability and applicable procedures. We provide guidance and support but do not guarantee admission irrespective of eligibility or availability.
